Procurement & Global Supply Chain Academy

Develop the knowledge, judgement and practical skills to manage procurement and supply chains from organisational need to final delivery, contract close-out and continuous improvement.

THE COMPLETE VALUE CHAIN

From need identification to value realisation

Procurement is not simply buying. It is an end-to-end management process that connects organisational strategy, markets, suppliers, contracts, logistics, finance, risk and customer outcomes.

IPSM's proposed learning architecture brings together operational competence, strategic decision-making and global supply-chain thinking. It is informed by recognised professional frameworks, including CIPS competency guidance, the ASCM SCOR model, ISM competencies and international public procurement guidance.

ACADEMY 01 · PROCUREMENT LIFECYCLE

The end-to-end procurement cycle

Students progressively learn each stage, the records it produces, the controls that protect it and the decisions that create value.

01

Needs identification & specifications

Stakeholder engagement, needs assessment, requisitions, market-informed specifications, terms of reference and statements of work.

02

Procurement planning & budgeting

Annual procurement plans, demand aggregation, budget alignment, procurement scheduling, approvals and procurement strategy.

03

Market intelligence & sourcing

Supply-market analysis, supplier mapping, category strategies, make-or-buy decisions, sourcing options and competition.

04

Solicitation & tendering

RFQs, RFPs, invitations to tender, tender documents, supplier communication, clarifications and secure submissions.

05

Evaluation & due diligence

Compliance checks, technical and financial evaluation, weighted criteria, risk-based due diligence and evaluation reports.

06

Negotiation & award

Negotiation planning, commercial analysis, approvals, award recommendations, notifications and supplier onboarding.

07

Contract lifecycle management

Contract formation, obligations, milestones, variations, claims, disputes, performance monitoring and close-out.

08

Delivery, logistics & receipt

Expediting, transport, receiving, inspection, quality acceptance, goods-received records and delivery performance.

09

Invoice, payment & reconciliation

Three-way matching, invoice verification, payment controls, reconciliation, documentation and financial accountability.

10

Disposal, returns & close-out

Asset disposal, returns, reverse logistics, contract closure, records retention and lessons learned.

11

Performance & value measurement

Procurement KPIs, savings validation, service levels, supplier scorecards, audit findings and benefits realisation.

12

Governance, ethics & assurance

Transparency, segregation of duties, conflicts of interest, internal controls, fraud indicators and professional conduct.

ACADEMY 02 · GLOBAL SUPPLY CHAIN

Understand the global movement of goods and services

Supply chains connect raw materials, manufacturers, service providers, ports, customs authorities, warehouses, distributors and end users. IPSM learners should understand how decisions in one part of the chain affect cost, service, resilience and sustainability elsewhere.

PlanSourceMakeDeliverReturnOrchestrate

Supply-chain strategy & planning

Supply-chain design, demand forecasting, sales and operations planning, capacity planning and network optimisation.

Inventory & warehouse management

ABC analysis, safety stock, reorder levels, EOQ, cycle counting, warehouse layout, WMS and stock accuracy.

International logistics & trade

Freight forwarding, shipping modes, Incoterms, import/export documentation, customs processes and trade compliance.

Transport & distribution

Fleet planning, route optimisation, carrier selection, last-mile delivery, distribution networks and service levels.

Supply-chain risk & resilience

Supplier dependency, geopolitical exposure, disruption planning, continuity strategies, scenario analysis and recovery.

Reverse & circular supply chains

Returns, repair, reuse, recycling, waste reduction, product lifecycle thinking and responsible disposal.

Skills for modern procurement leaders

Strategic sourcing & category management

Category segmentation, spend analysis, sourcing waves, supplier markets and strategic sourcing plans.

Commercial & financial skills

Total cost of ownership, lifecycle costing, price analysis, budgeting, cost modelling and value-for-money analysis.

Supplier relationship management

Supplier segmentation, performance scorecards, collaboration, development, innovation and relationship risk.

Digital procurement & ERP

E-sourcing, e-tendering, e-catalogues, e-contracts, ERP workflows, digital records and e-procurement controls.

Risk, compliance & assurance

Sustainable & ethical procurement

PROFESSIONAL LEARNING DESIGN

How IPSM programmes should be delivered

Professional capability grows when learners apply knowledge to realistic work. IPSM's proposed delivery model combines guided study with practical assessment.

Case-based learning

Use realistic public and private sector cases involving procurement planning, tendering, supplier failure and logistics challenges.

Simulated procurement files

Students prepare requisitions, specifications, tender packs, evaluation reports, purchase orders and contract registers.

Practical digital skills

Use spreadsheets, inventory templates, procurement dashboards and simulated e-procurement workflows.

Negotiation workshops

Role-play buyer and supplier negotiations, prepare negotiation plans and evaluate commercial outcomes.

Workplace projects

Analyse a procurement or supply-chain problem and propose a measurable improvement.

Capstone portfolio

Compile practical evidence of competence across the procurement cycle and supply-chain operations.
