Join IPSM

Start your professional learning journey through flexible online study and practical procurement and supply management programmes.

GENERAL ENTRY REQUIREMENT

Admission requirements

The general minimum admission requirement is five (5) Ordinary Level passes.

01

Minimum qualification

Applicants should have at least five (5) O-Level passes. Additional programme-specific requirements or relevant prior qualifications may apply for progression to higher levels.

02

Supporting documents

Prepare copies of your academic certificates or results, identification document, a recent passport-size photograph and any other documents requested by admissions.

03

Online learning

Students can enquire about online learning arrangements and access to approved reading materials and study resources.

APPLICATION PROCESS

How to apply

Choose your programme

Review the certificate, diploma, advanced diploma and professional short-course options.

Prepare your documents

Gather your five O-Level passes and supporting academic and identification documents.

Submit your online application

Complete the IPSM application form with accurate personal and academic information.

Await admissions guidance

The admissions team will review your enquiry and advise on the next steps, programme availability and any additional requirements.
