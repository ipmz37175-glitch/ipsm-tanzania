Applicant Document Upload

Supporting documents required for an IPSM application.

Important privacy notice — secure upload is not yet activated.
IPSM is preparing secure document storage for applicants. Until this notice is removed, the upload fields below are disabled. Please do not send identity documents, passports or birth certificates by ordinary email or WhatsApp. Contact admissions to ask about the approved submission method.

Documents to prepare

Prepare clear, readable scans or photographs. Ensure all four corners are visible and the document is not cut off.

  • Academic qualifications: certificates and transcripts relevant to your application.
  • National identity card: front and back, where applicable.
  • Passport: bio-data page, particularly for international applicants.
  • Birth certificate: where requested for identity or admission verification.

Accepted formats planned: PDF, JPG and PNG. Planned maximum: 2 MB per file and 8 MB total per submission. These are preliminary limits and will be confirmed when the secure service is configured.

Prepare your highest qualification and any other relevant academic records.

This page is currently an information and preparation page only. No files are uploaded or stored by this page.
