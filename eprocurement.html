TANZANIA CONTEXT

Understanding Tanzania's NeST system

The National e-Procurement System of Tanzania (NeST) supports e-registration, e-tendering, e-contract management, e-payment, e-catalogue and e-auction. IPSM learning can introduce the purpose, workflow and responsible use of these functions through guided case studies and practice exercises.

Important: NeST is a government-operated platform. IPSM is not claiming to own, operate, certify users on, or be officially affiliated with NeST or PPRA. Access, accounts, live tenders and official user training are controlled by the Government of Tanzania and PPRA.

INTERNATIONAL REFERENCE FRAMEWORKS

Learn against recognised global principles

These frameworks are used as learning references. They do not mean IPSM is accredited by, endorsed by, or authorised to issue certification on behalf of these organisations.

UNCITRAL Model Law on Public Procurement (2011)

Transparency, competition, fairness, objectivity, integrity, value for money and electronic communications in procurement.

Official UNCITRAL reference →

OECD Recommendation on Public Procurement

Integrated principles covering transparency, integrity, access, stakeholder participation, efficiency, e-procurement, capacity, evaluation, risk management and accountability.

Official OECD instrument →

World Bank Procurement Framework

Procurement planning, fit-for-purpose approaches, value for money, risk management, fairness and procurement for investment projects.

World Bank procurement →

ISO 20400 – Sustainable Procurement

Guidance for integrating sustainability into procurement decisions, supplier relationships and purchasing practices. It is guidance, not a certifiable management-system standard.

ISO 20400 information →

ISO 37001 – Anti-bribery management

Awareness of anti-bribery controls, due diligence, reporting, leadership commitment and organisational integrity.

ISO information →

ISO 31000 – Risk management

Risk identification, assessment, treatment, monitoring and communication applied to procurement and supply chains.

ISO information →

Applicable laws and regulations always take precedence. International frameworks are reference points and must be applied consistently with the relevant country's legislation, procurement rules and institutional policies.

PRACTICAL LEARNING MODULES

What learners can practise

Electronic tender workflow

Follow a simulated procurement from requisition and planning through tender publication, bid receipt, evaluation and award.

Supplier registration & bid readiness

Prepare supplier profiles, compliance checklists, tender response schedules and submission-readiness reviews.

Digital records & audit trails

Understand role-based access, approval trails, document control, data protection, cybersecurity awareness and record retention.

Procurement analytics

Use sample data to review spend, lead times, competition, savings, supplier performance and procurement KPIs.

Ethics, fraud risks & complaints

Identify conflicts of interest, bid-rigging warning signs, collusion risks, complaint procedures and accountability controls.

Contract performance

Monitor milestones, delivery, quality, variations, service levels, payment evidence and supplier performance.
