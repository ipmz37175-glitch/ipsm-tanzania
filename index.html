BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Questions worth asking early

A little clarity before an application makes the next conversation more useful.

Who are the programmes for?

Speak with IPSM admissions about your current experience, qualifications and study goals to identify the appropriate route.

How do I start an application?

Review the programme information, prepare your supporting details and use the online application page.

Can I speak to someone before applying?

Yes. Use the admissions conversation route or contact IPSM directly by WhatsApp or email.

How does certificate verification work?

Submit the certificate details through the verification page so the institute can review the request.
