CORE STUDY AREAS

Procurement and supply management

Programmes may cover the following subject areas, according to the selected qualification and approved curriculum.

Purchasing & Supply Management

Procurement planning, sourcing, purchasing procedures, supplier selection, negotiation and purchasing ethics.

Procurement & Logistics

Transport, distribution, warehousing, inventory control, stores administration and supply chain coordination.

Contract & Supplier Management

Contract administration, supplier performance, relationship management, compliance and risk control.

Digital Procurement & E-Government Procurement

Electronic tendering, supplier registration, digital bid submission, e-evaluation, e-contracts, e-catalogues, e-auctions, audit trails and procurement data.

Explore e-procurement learning →

Strategic & Sustainable Procurement

Category management, total cost of ownership, lifecycle costing, sustainable sourcing, risk analysis and value-for-money decisions.

Global Supply Chain & International Logistics

Global supply-chain planning, demand forecasting, freight forwarding, Incoterms, customs processes, transport, distribution and supply-chain resilience.

Procurement Lifecycle Management

End-to-end procurement from needs identification and planning through sourcing, tendering, evaluation, contract delivery, payment and close-out.

MODERN PROCUREMENT PRACTICE

Digital, transparent and internationally informed

Learning content is designed around modern procurement principles, including transparency, fair competition, integrity, value for money, sustainability, risk management and digital process control.

Explore e-procurement and standards
PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Short and professional courses

Focused courses for learners and working professionals who want to strengthen specific workplace skills. Short-course duration: 6 months.

Public Procurement & Tender Management

Procurement processes, tender documentation, evaluation principles and ethical practice.

Stores & Inventory Management

Stock control, inventory records, stocktaking, stores procedures and loss prevention.

Logistics & Supply Chain Management

Transport planning, distribution, warehousing and supply chain coordination.

Contract Management

Contract lifecycle, performance monitoring, documentation and risk management.

Supplier Relationship Management

Supplier appraisal, performance measurement, communication and continuous improvement.

Negotiation & Procurement Ethics

Negotiation techniques, professional conduct, transparency and conflict-of-interest awareness.

Electronic Public Procurement (e-GP)

Practical learning on digital procurement workflows, tender lifecycle controls, supplier portals, electronic records and compliance.

Procurement Data & Spend Analytics

Spend classification, dashboards, procurement KPIs, data quality, reporting and evidence-based decision-making.

Risk, Fraud Prevention & Internal Controls

Procurement risk registers, segregation of duties, red flags, audit trails, anti-bribery controls and reporting concerns.

Green & Sustainable Public Procurement

Environmental and social considerations, lifecycle costing, responsible sourcing and supplier sustainability assessment.

International Trade, Shipping & Incoterms

International purchasing, shipping documentation, freight options, customs procedures, trade terms and landed-cost analysis.

Demand Planning & Inventory Optimisation

Forecasting, reorder levels, safety stock, economic order quantity, stock classification and inventory performance.

ISO 9001:2015 – Quality Management Systems

Quality management principles, process approach, risk-based thinking, documented information, internal auditing, corrective action and continual improvement.
